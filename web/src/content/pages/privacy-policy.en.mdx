## Accounts and sessions

Registration stores the name and email you provide and a secure hash of your password. Necessary session cookies support sign-in. Session IP, browser information, language and registration attribution may also be recorded for account operation and security.

## Learning records

When you sign in to Tubitle in the same Chrome browser, the extension automatically uploads video word lookups, subtitle context, completed AI sentence analyses, video titles and playback positions. Anonymous queries stay local and are never backfilled after sign-in. Full viewing history, offline MDX files, dictionary content and personal provider keys are not uploaded.

Sync uses the website session directly. No connection code is required. Signing out or session expiry stops uploads. Pending signed-in records retry under their original account; signing out or changing accounts clears the old queue while preserving local history.

Records are isolated by account. Export your active records or delete individual records in My library. Deletion removes the saved content while retaining minimal identifiers and timestamps to prevent retries from recreating it. Existing backups are not immediately erased by that action.

## Third-party services

Source-video links open YouTube under its own data policies. The default Tubitle mode requires sign-in. Current and prefetched subtitle text passes through the website to the translation provider selected by the administrator: SiliconFlow, Alibaba Cloud Machine Translation, Microsoft Translator, or a self-hosted service. The administrator can change providers. Requesting AI analysis sends the sentence through the website to its configured AI provider. In personal API mode, subtitles and sentences go directly from the extension to the providers you configure; personal API keys are not uploaded to Tubitle and platform AI allowances are not consumed. Learning records still sync while signed in. For AI analysis, request identifiers, sentence hashes and daily usage are stored for accounting. Results are cached for same-day retries; expired caches are cleared on subsequent account requests. Analyses may separately be saved as learning records, which you can delete. Free accounts receive 3 analyses daily and Pro accounts 100, resetting at midnight Beijing time. Failures do not count. Syncing existing records does not invoke AI. OAuth, email, payment and analytics services operate only if configured and enabled by the site operator.

## Local development release

This delivery uses a local SQLite database and a local preview URL. It is not a public hosted service. Before public launch, the operator must supply contact details, actual hosting locations, retention periods and an account-deletion process.


## Learning podcasts and usage statistics

When you request a podcast or enable automatic generation, selected learning text and necessary context are sent to the configured text AI provider. Validated spoken scripts are sent to Google Cloud Text-to-Speech. You can pause automatic generation. Your podcast explanation language initially follows the webpage language at registration and can be changed in the membership center independently of extension translation preferences.

Successful platform translation counts and client-reported active playback time are aggregated by user and day without an additional per-sentence request log. Administrators with dedicated permissions can read these statistics and retained podcast transcripts, audio and listening progress. Statistics retain the latest 30 UTC day buckets. Device idempotency checkpoints retain at most seven days with a daily device limit.

Podcasts, scripts and associated playback records expire 30 days after creation. Access stops at expiry; maintenance removes audio and database content and retries storage failures. You may delete an episode sooner. Removing a source learning record also revokes associated podcasts and schedules cleanup. Language preferences, the used-trial marker and next-generation time survive content expiry. These rules do not automatically remove your original learning library. User-exported files and external backups cannot be immediately recalled.
